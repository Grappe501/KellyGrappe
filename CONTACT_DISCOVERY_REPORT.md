# Contact Discovery Report

## Ranked contact-like tables
- followups (score: 5)
- event_requests (score: 4)

## Operational conclusion
- `voters` is a targeting dataset, not a messaging-ready contact system.
- `contacts` exists by name but was not populated in the scanned sample.
- A production contact backbone should be built in `contacts` and linked to `voters` by `voter_id` when available.
